Privacy notice

How this public information website works, which services support it, and what happens when you contact GlobalFlowHub.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This website is a public overview of independent research and engineering work. It has no visitor accounts, user-submitted comment sections, or contact form.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and page performance. Our site's analytics integration does not set analytics cookies. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data for Web Analytics. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Website hosting

This site is served using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that, for security purposes, it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses when a GitHub Pages site is accessed. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Contacting GlobalFlowHub

If you email or call us through the public contact details shown on this website, we may receive your contact information and any details you choose to share. We use that information to read, respond to, and follow up on your inquiry. The email or phone services involved may process communications under their own terms and privacy policies.

Please do not send credentials, private records, sensitive internal system details, or other confidential information through ordinary email unless a suitably secure method has been agreed upon.

Search visibility and third-party links

We use Google Search Console to understand how this public website appears in Google Search. Search Console ownership verification does not add a visitor-tracking script to this website. Links to outside services take you to websites with their own privacy practices.

Sharing and changes

We do not sell information submitted through this website. Hosting, analytics, and communication service providers may handle relevant information to provide their services. We may revise this notice as the site or its services change and update the date shown above.

Questions
